> **AMENDMENT IN:** SECURITIES ACT, 2063

> *A provision has been added allowing a Ministry of Finance representative on the board to serve as chairperson in case of a vacancy.*

### Section 7: Appointment of Chairperson (Sub-section 8 added after sub-section 7)

Notwithstanding anything contained elsewhere in this Act, **the member representing the Ministry of Finance on the Board shall serve as the Chairman of the Board** in case:
* the position of the Chairman becomes vacant,
* the Government of Nepal removes him from office in accordance with sub-section (7) i.e. act against the interest of the board or development of capital market or causes any loss to the board, or
* he is unable to perform the functions of the Board due to his suspension.

#### AMENDMENT IN: LABOUR ACT, 2074

> *The age for compulsory retirement of a worker has been increased to 60 years.*

### Section 147: Compulsory Retirement
`Old Provision`
Any worker in regular employment, after completing the age of 58 years, shall retire compulsorily. Provided that if there is a need to give retirement to workers before the prescribed age of 58 for special nature of work, a provision to that effect may be made in the by-law by acquiring approval from the council as prescribed.	

`New Provision`
Any worker in regular employment, after completing the age of **60 years**, shall retire compulsorily. Provided that if there is a need to give retirement to workers before the prescribed age of **60 for special nature of work**, a provision to that effect may be made in the by-law by acquiring  **approval from the council as prescribed.**
