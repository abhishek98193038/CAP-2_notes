### Partners' Capital A/c
 * Fixed Capital A/c
 * Fluctuating Capital A/c

1. Fixed Capital
	Two capital accounts are maintained:
	 * Permanent Capitals are included in Capital A/c as well as Capital Introduced or Capital Withdrawn.
	 * Changes in capital such as share of profit, interest on capital, salary to partner's etc. are included in Current A/c.
2. Fluctuating Capital
	 * Only one capital a/c is maintained.
	 * All items are included in one capital a/c.
> Note:
>  * If Balance sheet has Capital A/c and Current A/c, it is Fixed Capital System.
>  * If Balance sheet has only Capital A/c, it is Fluctuating Capital A/c.
> 

### Calculation of Interest on Drawing
 * Calculated only if mentioned in partnership deed.
 * Interest is calculated from date of drawing to date of Balance Sheet.
 * If date of drawing is not given, interest is calculated for 6 months.

### Calculation of Interest on Capital
 * Interest on capital is calculated on capital present at the year beginning without any adjustment except for capital withdrawn or introduced.
Note:
 1. If there is Loss:
   * If there is loss from P/L A/c, partners are not allowed for interest on capital, salary, etc.
 
 2. If there is Insufficient Profit:
   * (i.e., profit is lower than the total amount of interest on capital, salary payable to partners)
   * Profit is distributed to partners in the ratio of Interest on Capital and Salary.
Example:
 * Salary: A = 10,000, B = 5,000
 * Interest: A = 1,000, B = 15,000
 * Total  A = 11,000 B = 20,000
 * Actual Profit: 15,000 (from P/L A/c)
Solution:
Distribute profit in 11:20 ratio.

1. If there is Sufficient Profit
 * Pay all interests and salaries, then distribute remaining profit in Profit Sharing Ratio (PSR).

### Accounting of Goodwill
Three methods of treatment:
 * Goodwill is raised.
 * Goodwill is raised and written off.
 * Goodwill is adjusted through capital A/c.
Example:

* $\text{Goodwill} = \text{Rs. } 30,000$ *
* $\text{Old Partner PSR} = 7:2$ 
* $\text{New Partner PSR} = 2:2:1$ 

 #### Method 1: $$\text{Goodwill A/c \quad Dr. \quad 20,000}$$  $$\text{To A's Capital \quad 18,000}$$  $$\text{To B's Capital \quad 2,000}$$  #### Method 2: $$\text{Goodwill A/c \quad Dr. \quad 20,000}$$  $$\text{To A's Capital \quad 18,000}$$  $$\text{To B's Capital \quad 2,000}$$  
 
 
 
 $$\text{A's Capital A/c \quad Dr. \quad 12,000}$$  $$\text{B's Capital A/c \quad Dr. \quad 12,000}$$  $$\text{C's Capital A/c \quad Dr. \quad 6,000}$$ $$\text{To Goodwill A/c \quad 20,000}$$  --- # Page 6 #### Method 3:
  Entry of Method 2 in working note, and net entry in answer:$$\text{i.e. C's Capital A/c \quad Dr. \quad 6,000}$$  $$\text{To A's Capital A/c \quad 6,000}$$



#### Which Method to Use:
 * We do not apply Method 1 unless permissible.
 * Method 2 & 3 can be applied at choice.
* Method 1 can be applied if:
     * Goodwill already appearing in Balance Sheet.

### Adjustment of profit sharing ratio $PSR$


**1. When the new share of the incoming partner is given but sacrifice of the old partners are not given**

Illustration 1:
A and B are partners sharing profits in the ratio of 3:2. They admit C for 1/5th share as new
partner. Calculate new profit sharing ratio and sacrificing ratio of old partners.

a$ New Profit Sharing ratio: 
Let the total profit be = 1 
C‘s share = $1/5$ 
Remaining share of A and B = 1-1/5 =4/5 

Old Ratio of A = $3/5$ 
New Share of A = $4/5 \times 3/5 =12/25$ 

Old Ratio of B = $2/5$ 
New Share of B = $4/5 \times 2/5 = 8/25$ 

Old Share of C= $1/5$ 
New share of C = $1/5 \times 5/5 = 5/25$ 

New Profit Sharing Ration $A,B,C$ = 12: 8: 5

b) Sacrifice ratio 
Sacrifice = Old share- New Share 
A‘s Sacrifice = $3/5-12/25 =3/25$ 
B‘s Sacrifice = $2/5- 8/25 =2/25$ 
Sacrifice ratio $A:B$ =$(3/25:2/25)$

**2. Unequal sacrifice of the old partners (Unequal sacrifice)**

Illustration 2: 
A and B are partners sharing profits in the ratio of 3:2. They admit C for 1/5th share of future profits which C acquires 3/20th from A and 1/20th from B. Calculate new Profit sharing ratio and sacrificing ratio of old partners.

Solution

a. New Profit Sharing ratio: 
Old share of A = $3/5$ 
Sacrifice of A =$3/20$ 
New Share of A = $3/5-3/20 =9/20$

Old share of B = $2/5$ 
Sacrifice of B = $1/20$ 
New share of B=$2/5-1/20 =7/20$ 

Share of C = $1/5$ 
New share of C=$1/5 \times 4/4 = 4/20$ 

New Ratio = 9:7:4

b. Sacrifice ratio Sacrifice ratio $A:B$ =$(3/20:1/20)$

**3. Equal sacrifice of the old partners (Equal sacrifice)**

Illustration 3: 
A and B are partners sharing profits in the ratio of 3:2. They admit C for 1/5th Share which acquires equally from A and B. Calculate new profit sharing ratio and sacrificing ratio of old partners.

Solution:

a) New Profit sharing ratio 
Old share of A=$3/5$ 
Sacrifice of A on share of C = $1/5 \times 1/2 =1/10$ 
New Share of A = $3/5-1/10 =5/10$ 
Old share of B=$2/5$ 
Sacrifice of B on share of C = $1/5 \times 1/2 =1/10$ 
New Share of B = $2/5-1/10 =3/10$ 
Share of C =$1/5$ 
New share of C =$1/5 \times 2/2 =2/10$ 
New Profit sharing ratio $A:B:C$ = 5:3:2

**4. Entire Sacrifice by one partner only**

Illustration 4: 
A and B are partners sharing profits in the ratio of 3:2. They admit C for 1/5th share which he acquires entirely from A. Calculate a$ new ratio and b$ Sacrificing ratio.

Solution:

a) New Profit sharing ratio 
Old share of A = $3/5$ 
Sacrifice of A on share of C = $1/5$ 
New Share of A = $3/5 - 1/5 =2/5$ 
Old share of B = $2/5$ 
New Share of B = $2/5$ 
Share of C =$1/5$ 
New share of C =$1/5$ 
New Profit sharing ratio $A:B:C$ = 2:2:1

b. Sacrifice ratio Sacrifice ratio $A$ =$1/5$

**5. When the new share of the incoming partner is not given.**

Illustration 5: A and B are partners sharing profits in the ratio of 3:2. They admit C as new partner which he acquires 1/5th of A‘s share and 2/5 of B‘s share. Calculate a$ New ratio and b$ Sacrificing ratio.

Solution:

a) New Profit sharing ratio 
Old share of A=$ 3/5$ 
Sacrifice of A on share of C = $3/5 \times 1/5 =3/25$ 
New Share of A =$3/5 – 3/25 =12/25$ 
Old share of B=$ 2/5$ 
Sacrifice of B on share of C = $2/5 \times 2/5 =4/25$ 
New Share of B = $2/5-4/25 =6/25$ 
New share of C =$3/25 + 4/25 =7/25$ 
New Profit sharing ratio $A:B:C$ = 12:6:7

b. Sacrifice ratio 
Sacrifice ratio $A:B$ =$3/25:4/25$

**6. When the new share of all the partner is given.**

Illustration 6: X and Y are partners sharing profits in the ratio of 3:2. They admit Z as a new partner. The new profit sharing ratio among X, Y and Z is 4:3:2. Find out the sacrificing ratio.

Solution:

a) New Profit sharing ratio 
New share of Z =$2/9$ 
New Profit sharing ratio $X:Y:Z$ = 4:3:2

b. Sacrifice ratio 
Old share of X = $3/5$ 
New Share of X =$4/9$ 
Sacrifice of X = $3/5 -4/9 = 7/45$

Old share of Y = $2/5$ 
New Share of Y =$3/9$ 
Sacrifice of Y =$2/5- 3/9 =3/45$ 
Sacrifice ratio $X:Y$ =$7/45:3/45$

**7. Illustration 7:**

X and Y are in partnership sharing profits and losses at the ratio of 5:3. They take Z as a new partner. Calculate the new profit sharing ratio if-

$i.$ Z purchases 1/10 share from X. 
$ii.$ X and Y agree to sacrifice 1/10 share to Z in the ratio of 2:3. 
$iii.$ Simply Z gets 1/10 share of profit.

Solution:

$i$ New Profit Sharing ratio X $5/8-1/10 = 21/40$ Y $3/8$ 
i.e. $15/40$ Z $1/10$ 
i.e. $4/40$ 
Therefore, X:Y:Z=21:15:4

$ii$ X's Sacrifice $1/10 \times 2/5 = 2/50$ 
Y's Sacrifice $1/10 \times 3/5 = 3/50$ 
New profit sharing ratio. X $5/8 – 2/50 = 117/200$ Y $3/8 – 3/50 = 63/200$ Z $1/10 = 20/200$ 
i.e. 117:63:20

$iii$ Balance share to be divided between X and Y $1-1/10 = 9/10$ 
Distribution: X $9/10 \times 5/8 = 45/80$ Y $9/10 \times 3/8 = 27/80$ Z $1/10 = 8/80$ 
i.e. 45:27:8




### Valuation/ Adjustment of Goodwill 

Goodwill exists only when the firm earns super profits. Any firm that earns normal profits
or is incurring losses has no goodwill.
There are two types of goodwill
1. *Purchase Goodwill* :
2. *Self generated or inherent Goodwill* : It is internally generated goodwill for any specific purpose like change in profit sharing ratios among the existing partners, new admission of partner, retirement of partner etc. This type of goodwill normally write-off immediately after necessary adjustment of partners' capital accounts

<u><strong>Interest on drawing</strong></u>

a) **If the fixed amount is withdrawn in the beginning of every month:**
Take the period at the average of the periods applicable to the first and last installment.
The period of first installment is 12 months
The period of last installment is 1 month.
Average period = $12 + 1$ / 2
= 6½ months

b) **If the fixed amount is withdrawn in the middle of every month:**
Take the period at the average of the periods applicable to the first and last installment.
The period of first installment is 11½ months.
The period of last installment is ½ month.
Average period = $11½ + ½$ / 2
= 6 months

c) **If the fixed amount is withdrawn at the end of every month:**
Take the period at the average of the periods applicable to the first and last installment.
The period of first installment is 11 months.
The period of last installment is 0 month.
Average period = $11 + 0$ / 2
= 5½ months

d)**When the dates of drawings are not given:**
The average of the total period is to be taken
Average Period = Total period /2

1) If the drawings are for one year without dates, the average period is calculated as follows:
Average Period = 12 months/2
= 6 months

2) If the drawings are for 6 months without dates, then,
Average Period = 6 months/2
= 3 months
