## Question – 7

SK Ltd. is setting up a project with a capital outlay of ₹60,00,000. It has two alternatives in financing the project cost.

Alternative – 1: 100% equity finance by issuing equity shares of ₹10 each.

Alternative -II; Debt-equity ratio 2:1 (issuing equity shares of ₹10 each)  

The rate of interest payable on the debt is 18% p.a. The corporate tax rate is 40%. Calculate the indifference point between the two alternative methods of financing.

The difference point between the two alternatives is calculated by:

$$
\frac{(\mathrm{EBIT} - \mathrm{I}_1)(1 - T)}{E_1} = \frac{(\mathrm{EBIT} - \mathrm{I}_2)(1 - T)}{E_2}
$$

$$
\frac{(\mathrm{EBIT} - 0)(1 - 0.40)}{6,00,000} = \frac{(\mathrm{EBIT} - 7,20,000)(1 - 0.40)}{2,00,000}
$$

$$
\frac{(\mathrm{EBIT})(0.60)}{6,00,000} = \frac{(\mathrm{EBIT} - 7,20,000)(0.60)}{2,00,000}
$$

$$
\frac{\mathrm{EBIT}(0.60)}{3} = \frac{0.60(\mathrm{EBIT} - 7,20,000)}{1}
$$

EBIT = 3EBIT - 21,60,000

 \- 2 EBIT = -21,60,000

EBIT = $\frac{21,60,000}{2}$

EBIT = ₹ 10,80,000

Therefore, at EBIT of ₹ 10,80,000 earnings per share for the two alternatives is equal.

## Question – 9

SK Ltd. is considering a new project which requires a capital investment of ₹ 9 crores. Interest on term loan is 12% and corporate tax rate is 30%. Calculate the point of indifference for the project considering the Debt Equity ratio insisted by the financing agencies being 2:1.

$$
Option - 1) \quad Equity = 9 \text{ Lr.}
$$

$$
Option - 2) \quad Debt = \frac{2}{3} \times 9 \text{ Lr.} = 6 \text{ Lr.}
$$

$$
Equity = 3 \text{ Lr.}
$$

<u>Answer</u>

Point of Indifference between the above two alternatives =

$$
\frac{\text{EBIT} \times (1 - t)}{\text{No. of equity shares} \times (N_1)} = \frac{(\text{EBIT} - \text{Interest}) \times (1 - t)}{\text{No. of equity shares} \times (N_2)}
$$

$$
\text{Or, } \frac{\text{EBIT} \times (1 - 0.30)}{90,00,000 \text{ shares}} = \frac{(\text{EBIT} - 72,00,000) \times (1 - 0.30)}{30,00,000 \text{ shares}}
$$

$$
\text{Or, } 0.7 \text{ EBIT} = 2.1 \text{ EBIT} - 1,51,20,000
$$

$$
\text{EBIT} = 1,08,00,000
$$

EBIT at point of Indifference will be 1.08 crore.

(The face value of the equity shares is assumed as 10 per share. However, indifference point will be the same irrespective of face value per share).

## Question - 10

SK Ltd. is considering two alternative financing plans as follows:

| Particulars                     | Plan – A (₹) | Plan – B (₹) |
| ------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------ |
| Equity shares of ₹ 10 each      | 8,00,000     | 8,00,000     |
| Preference Shares of ₹ 100 each | -            | 4,00,000     |
| 12% Debentures                  | 4,00,000     | -            |
|                                 | 12,00,000    | 12,00,000    |

The indifference point between the plans is ₹ 4,80,000. Corporate tax rate is 30%. Calculate the rate of dividend on preference shares.

<u>Answer</u>

**Computation of Rate of Preference Dividend**

$$
\frac{(\text{EBIT - Interest}) (1 - t)}{N_o \text{ of Equity Shares } (N_1)} = \frac{\text{EBIT } (1 - t) - \text{Preference Dividend}}{N_o \text{ of Equity Shares } (N_2)}
$$

$$
\frac{(\text{₹ 4,80,000 - ₹ 48,000}) \times (1 - 0.30)}{80,000 \text{ shares}} = \frac{\text{₹ 4,80,000 } (1 - 0.30) - \text{Preference Dividend}}{80,000 \text{ shares}}
$$

$$
\frac{\text{₹ 3,02,400}}{80,000 \text{ shares}} = \frac{\text{₹ 3,36,000 - Preference Dividend}}{80,000 \text{ shares}}
$$

$$
\text{₹ 3,02,400} = \text{₹ 3,36,000 - Preference Dividend}
$$

$$
\text{Preference Dividend} = \text{₹ 3,36,000 - ₹ 3,02,400} = \text{₹ 33,600}
$$

$$
\text{Rate of Dividend} = \frac{\text{Preference Dividend}}{\text{Preference share capital}} \times 100
$$

$$
= \frac{\text{₹ 33,600}}{4,00,000} \times 100 = 8.4\%
$$

## Question - 23

Determine the optimal capital structure of a company from the following information:

|  Options | Cost of Debt (Kd) in % | Cost of Equity (Ke) in % | Percentage of Debt on total value (Debt + Equity)  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  1 | 11.0 | 13.0 | 0.0  |
|  2 | 11.0 | 13.0 | 0.1  |
|  3 | 11.6 | 14.0 | 0.2  |
|  4 | 12.0 | 15.0 | 0.3  |
|  5 | 13.0 | 16.0 | 0.4  |
|  6 | 15.0 | 18.0 | 0.5  |
|  7 | 18.0 | 20.0 | 0.6  |


<u>Answer</u>

Note that the ratio given in this question is not debt to equity ratio. Rather it is the debt to total value ratio. Therefore, if the ratio is 0.6, it means that capital employed comprises 60% debt and 40% equity.

$$
K_{0} = \frac{K_{d} \times D + K_{e} \times S}{D + S}
$$

In this question total of weight is equal to 1 in all cases, hence we need not to divide by it.

1)  $K_{0} = 11\% \times 0 + 13\% \times 1 = 13.00\%$
2)  $K_{0} = 11\% \times 0.1 + 13\% \times 0.9 = 12.80\%$
3)  $K_{0} = 11.6\% \times 0.2 + 14\% \times 0.8 = 13.52\%$
4)  $K_{0} = 12\% \times 0.3 + 15\% \times 0.7 = 14.10\%$
5)  $K_{0} = 13\% \times 0.4 + 16\% \times 0.6 = 14.80\%$
6)  $K_{0} = 15\% \times 0.5 + 18\% \times 0.5 = 16.50\%$
7)  $K_{0} = 18\% \times 0.6 + 20\% \times 0.4 = 18.80\%$

Decision: $2^{\text{nd}}$ option is the best because it has lowest WACC.

|  Value of the firm (V) = $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{k_0} = \frac{\text{₹ } 5,00,000}{0.15}$ | 33,33,333  |

(ii) Calculation of cost of equity

|   | ₹  |
| --- | --- |
|  Market value of debt (D) | 15,00,000  |
|  Market value of equity (S) = V - D = ₹ 33,33,333 - ₹ 15,00,000 | 18,33,333  |


$$
K_e = \frac{\text{Earnings available for equity holders}}{\text{Value of equity (S)}}
$$

$$
\mathrm{Or}_t = \frac{\text{EBIT} - \text{Interest paid on debt}}{\text{Market value of equity}} = \frac{\₹3,50,000}{\₹18,33,333} \approx 19.09\%
$$

OR

$$
K_o = K_e \left( \frac{S}{V} \right) + K_d \left( \frac{D}{V} \right)
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
K_e = K_o \left( \frac{V}{S} \right) - K_d \left( \frac{D}{S} \right) \\
= 0.15 \left( \frac{33,33,333}{18,33,333} \right) - 0.10 \left( \frac{15,00,000}{18,33,333} \right) \\
= \frac{1}{18,33,333} \left[ (0.15 \times 33,33,333) - (0.10 \times 15,00,000) \right] \\
= \frac{1}{18,33,333} \left[ 5,00,000 - 1,50,000 \right] = 19.09\%
\end{aligned}
$$
