2) Expected earnings per share at the end of the year: ₹ 10 (£1)
3) Dividend Payout ratio: 80% → D₁ = 10 × 80% = 8
4) Growth Rate: 6% (£3)
5) Rate of return on risk free investment: 8% (RF)
6) Rate of return on market portfolio: 18% (RM)
7) Volatility of securities return relative to the return of a broad based market portfolio: 1.275

<u>Rough</u>

a) $ke = \frac{D}{P_0} = \frac{8}{100} = 0.08 = 8\%$
b) $ke = \frac{D_1}{P_0} + g = \frac{8}{100} + 0.06 = 14\%$
c) $ke = \frac{EPS}{P_0} = \frac{10}{100} = 10\%$
d) $ke = \frac{EPS_1}{P_0} + g = \frac{10}{100} + 0.06 = 16\%$
e) $ke = 8 + (1e-8)(1.275) = 20.75\%$


<u>Answer</u>

(a) Dividend Price Approach

$$
\mathrm{Ke} = \frac{D_1}{P_0} = \frac{80\% \text{ of } 10}{100} = 8\%
