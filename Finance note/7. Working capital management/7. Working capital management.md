
# working capital 

- funds required for day today operations 
- in other words, it is like blood in the human body 

A. Working capital on the basis of investment 
1. Investment in current assets (CA) => Gross working capital 
2. CA less CL => net working capital 

B. Working capital on the basis of time 
3. Permanent working capital 
4. Temporary working capital

Sourcing of working capital 

|               | Conservative (risk averser) | Moderate   | Aggressive(risk taker) |
| ------------- | --------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------- |
| Permanent WC  | Long term                   | Long term  | Long term + short term |
| Temporary WC  | Long term + short term      | Short term | Short term             |
| Profitability | Low                         | Moderate   | High                   |
| Liquidity     | High                        | Moderate   | Low                    |

Statement of working capital estimation 

Particulars
$$\text{Raw material} \left[ \frac{\text{Annual Raw material consumption}}{\text{365d/52w/12m}} \times \text{RM stock period} \right] $$
$$\text{WIP stock} \left[ \left( \frac{\text{RM annual consumption}}{\text{365d/52w/12m}} \times \text{WIP Period} \times DOC \right) + \left( \frac{\text{RM annual consumption}}{\text{365d/52w/12m}} \times \text{Conversion cost} \times DOC \right) \right]$$
$$ \text{Finished goods stock} \left[ \frac{\text{COP/COGS}}{\text{365d/52w/12m}} \times \text{FG stock period} \right] $$
$$ \text{Debtors} \left[ \frac{\text{Credit COS}}{\text{365d/52w/12m}} \times \text{Average collection period} \right] $$
$$ \text{Prepaid Expenses } \left[ \frac{\text{Expenses}}{\text{365d/52w/12m}} \times \text{Prepaid period} \right]$$
Cash and bank balance

<u>Current liabilities</u>

$$ \text{Creditors for material} \left[ \frac{\text{Credit purchase}}{\text{365d/52w/12m}} \times \text{credit period} \right] $$
$$ \text{Creditors for expense (outstanding expense)} \left[ \frac{\text{ expense}}{\text{365d/52w/12m}} \times \text{outstanding period} \right]$$

Notes:
1. DOC = degree of completion 
2. Conversion cost = cost to convert raw material into finished goods 
   = Direct labor + direct expense + factory overheads 
3. Unless otherwise provided, in case of WIP, DOC of raw material is assumed at 100% and DOC of conversion cost will be assumed at 50% 
4. funds blocked in debtor is always on the basis of cost involved
5. Working capital estimation 
	1. Total basis (default) : we take all expenses including non cash expenses (i.e. depreciation )
	2. Cash cost basis (apply only if asked in question) : we take only cash expense and ignore non cash expense
