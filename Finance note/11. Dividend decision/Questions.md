
## Question - 1

SK Ltd. earns ₹10 per share. Capitalization rate and return on investment are 10% and 12% respectively. Determine the optimum dividend payout ratio and the price of the share at the payout.

Sol) Given $\Sigma PS = \Sigma = Rs.10$

Cost of Equity = $Ke = 10\%$

Rate of Return = $\gamma = 12\%$

Since $\gamma(12%) &gt; Ke(10\%)$

$\therefore$ As per Walter Model, optimal payout ratio = $0\%$

$\therefore \Delta PS = \Delta = 10 \times 0\% = Rs.0$

$$
\begin{array}{l}
P_0 = \frac{\Delta + (\Sigma - \Delta)(\frac{\gamma}{Ke})}{\Delta} \\
= \frac{0 + (10 - \gamma)(\frac{0.12}{0.10})}{0.10} \\
= Rs.120 \\
\end{array}
$$

## Question - 8

SK Ltd. is a company having share capital of ₹ 10 lakhs of ₹ 10 each. It distributed current dividend of 20% per annum. Annual growth rate in dividend expected is 2%. The expected rate of return on its equity capital is 15%. Calculate price of share applying Gordon's growth model.

Sol) $D_0 = 10 \times 20\% = k_2$

$$
\begin{array}{l}
D_1 = 2(1 + g) = 2(1 + 0.02) = 2.04 \\
g = 2\% = 0.02 \\
k_e = 15\% = 0.15 \\
P_0 = \frac{\sum (1 - b)}{k_e - g} = \frac{D_1}{k_e - g} = \frac{2.04}{0.15 - 0.02} = k_1 15.69 \\
\end{array}
$$

## Question - 17

The earnings per share of a company is $\overline{\pm}30$ and dividend payout ratio is $60\%$. Multiplier is 2. Determine the price per share as per Graham &amp; Dodd Model.

$$
\begin{array}{l}
\text{Sol} \quad \sum PS = \sum = k_{s.30} \\
\Delta PS = \Delta = 30 \times 60\% = k_{s.18} \\
m = 2 \\
p_0 = m \left[ \Delta + \frac{\sum}{2} \right] \\
= 2f_{18} + \left[ \frac{30}{2} \right] \\
= k_{s.56} \\
\end{array}
$$
