
## Question - 1

A company’s requirements for ten days 6,300 units. The ordering cost per order is ₹ 10 and the carrying cost per unit is ₹ 0.26. You are required to calculate the economic order quantity.


<u>Rough</u>
→ 10 days
A = 6,300 units
O = Rs. 10
C = Rs. 0.26

EDQ = $\frac{\sqrt{2 \times A \times C}}{C} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 6,300 \times 10}{0.26}} = 696$ units

## Question - 3

SK company is a distributor of air filters to retail stores. It buys its filters from several manufacturers. Filters are ordered in lot sizes of 1,000 and each order costs ₹ 40 to place. Demand from retail stores is 20,000 filters per month, and carrying cost is ₹ 0.10 a filter per month.

(a) Compute the optimal order quantity with respect to so many lot sizes?
(b) Calculate the optimal order quantity if the carrying cost were ₹ 0.05 a filter per month?
(c) Compute the optimal order quantity if ordering costs were ₹ 10?

<u>Rough</u>

$$
\begin{array}{r l}
\boxed{(a)} &amp; A = \frac{20\,000 \times 12}{1\,000} = 240 \\
&amp; O = R_{3.40} \\
&amp; C = R_{0.10} \times 12 \times 1\,000 = R_{3.1200} \\
&amp; \Sigma_{OD} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 240 \times 40}{1\,200}} \\
&amp; = 4
\end{array}
\quad
\begin{array}{r l}
\boxed{(a)} &amp; A = 240 \\
&amp; O = R_{3.40} \\
&amp; C = 0.05 \times 12 \times 1\,000 = 600 \\
&amp; \Sigma_{OD} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 240 \times 40}{600}} \\
&amp; = 5.66
\end{array}
\quad
\begin{array}{r l}
\boxed{(a)} &amp; A = 240 \\
&amp; O = R_{3.10} \\
&amp; C = R_{0.1200} \\
&amp; \Sigma_{OD} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 240 \times 10}{1\,200}} \\
&amp; = 2
\end{array}
$$
