# Time value of money

Financial Mathematics Formulas

Interest & Compounding
 * Simple Interest: $P \times T \times r$
 * Compound Amount: $P(1 + r)^n$
 * Compound Interest: $P[(1 + r)^n - 1]$
 * Compound Interest (m periods per year): $P \left[ \left( 1 + \frac{r}{m} \right)^{nm} - 1 \right]$
 * Compound Amount (Continuous): $Pe^{rt},$ where $e \approx 2.7182$
 * Effective Rate: $\left( 1 + \frac{r}{m} \right)^m - 1$
Depreciation
 * Depreciated Amount (S): $V(1 - r)^n$
Annuities
 * Future Value (Ordinary Annuity): $\frac{C}{r} [(1 + r)^n - 1]$
 * Future Value (Annuity Due): $\frac{C}{r} [(1 + r)^n - 1](1 + r)$
 * Present Value (Ordinary Annuity): $\frac{C}{r} [1 - (1 + r)^{-n}]$
 * Present Value (Annuity Due): $\frac{C}{r} [1 - (1 + r)^{-n}](1 + r)$
Perpetuity
 * Present Value of Perpetuity: $\frac{P}{r} or \frac{C}{r}$

## Modigiliani and miller approach 
#Modigiliani_and_Miller_approach ![[finance note (1).pdf#page=3&rect=7,172,571,768|finance note (1), p.3]]


![[finance note (1).pdf#page=4&rect=78,576,535,733|finance note (1), p.4]]

<u>Assumptions</u>
1. Perfect market  and rational investor
2. No tax on income
3. Company had already decided the year end capital expenditure
4. $K_{e} = r$

>[!note]
> $K_{e}$ = cost of capital = rate of return of market = $\frac{r_{1} + r_{2} + r_{3} \dots\dots r_{n} }{n}$ 
> where,
> $r_{1}$ = rate of return of 1 company in the market 
> n = no. of companies in the market
>
> and
>
> r = rate of return of our company = Dividend + retained earning

\[ (1) investors are rational and they know price wont change in perfect market and they don't buy or sell shares due to dividend decision . Therefore dividend decision is irrelevant in MM theory]
$$P_{1} = P_{0}(1 + K_{e}) - D$$ Assume dividend is not distributed
$$P_{1} = P_{0}(1 + K_{e})$$\[multiplying both sides by $n_{0}$ =  no. of old shares]
$$n_{0}P_{1} = n_{0} P_{0}(1 + K_{e})$$
\[After issuing new share for investment in business/project]
\[ (3) company's capital expenditure is already decided so company will issue necessary shares to acquire investments through income earned + extra investment from issue of new shares]
$$\text{investment acquired  } + n_{0}P_{1} = n_{0} P_{0}(1 + K_{e}) + \text{investment acquired}$$
\[ (1) since, this is a perfect market , price does not change even after increasing the supply by issuing new shares]
$$n_{1}P_{1} + n_{0}P_{1} = n_{0} P_{0}(1 + K_{e}) + \text{Investment - Earning available}$$
$$( n_{1} + n_{0})P_{1} = n_{0} P_{0}(1 + K_{e}) + I - E$$
\[since, $n_{0}P_{0} = V_{0} = \text{old value of company}$]
$$( n_{1} + n_{0})P_{1} = V_{0}(1 + K_{e}) + I - E$$

$$ V_{0} = \frac{( n_{1} + n_{0})P_{1} - I + E}{(1 + K_{e})} $$

# Capital budgeting 
<U>objective</u>
- long term investment decision
- fixed asset investment decision

<U>Importance</u>
- Huge funds are involved
- Irreversible in short term
- Future return of business depends on this investment
